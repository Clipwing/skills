# Script checklist

The team's review comments on generated videos (October 2026), turned into checks on the script. Each item is a yes/no question on the beat sheet.

## Story

- [ ] One angle, and every beat serves it. Test: drop the beat. If the video loses nothing, cut it.
- [ ] The first beat is the hook: in 2–3 s it says what the viewer gets or opens a question. It starts with the substance, not a greeting or a logo.
- [ ] Beginning, middle, end. The payoff answers the hook.
- [ ] **Sound-off read**: read only the "On screen" column top to bottom. The story is clear from it alone.
- [ ] Promo: the product is on screen within the first third, and its screens show the steps that solve this video's problem. A tour of the whole product is a different video.
- [ ] Explainer: the video explains the article's idea. It does not show the article itself with highlights.

## Facts

- [ ] Every number, name and example is on the fact sheet, with the same meaning ("6 years making games" stays that, never "6 years old"). That includes the words inside visuals: labels, chips, post texts, screen states.
- [ ] The author's person is kept: "I" stays "I", not "he".
- [ ] Brands mentioned get their logo in the visual; a large sum gets a counter.

## Text

- [ ] Numbers as digits: 40, 2, 10x.
- [ ] Plain, specific words a creator would say to a friend. Ad-speak ("unlock", "game-changer", "revolutionize", "in today's world") is rewritten.
- [ ] Captions are whole phrases of 2–7 words; on-screen text is key words, never a paragraph.
- [ ] One emphasis per beat, on the part of the phrase that carries the meaning.
- [ ] Titles end without a full stop; no emoji; no italics unless the brand uses them.

## Picture

- [ ] Every beat names one concrete focal object: a product screen, a phone with a Short, a logo row, a number. No placeholders.
- [ ] The layout changes at least every 3–5 s, and no visual type appears more than 3 times.
- [ ] The simplest version that works: one focal point per frame, as a motion designer would do it by hand.
- [ ] Elements that belong together stay on screen together; a new block appears only after the previous one is read.

## Timing

- [ ] The beats add up to the target length.
- [ ] Silent: every caption has at least 0.5 s + 1 s per 3 words. Voice: 2.5 words per second, and each beat holds about 1 s after its line.
- [ ] Calm pace: fewer, longer beats beat many short ones.

## Sound

- [ ] Every sound is the real-world sound of an action on screen (click, pop, typing, paper), and they vary. Whooshes, swooshes and "thwips" on motion are replaced or dropped.
- [ ] With a voiceover: the script asks the user to listen to the generated voice before the build (clicks and breaths get regenerated).
