## What it is

"Send a video link and a real editor turns it into ready-to-post Shorts, Reels and TikToks in 3 days. Smarter than AI clippers, easier than hiring."

- A real, vetted editor makes the clips, hand-picked for the client. Auto-clippers give generic cuts you still have to fix.
- Input: any video on YouTube (podcasts, webinars, demos, product updates) or a Loom link. No timecodes needed: finding the best moments is the editor's job.
- Output: vertical clips with captions, custom fonts and branding, back within 3 days.
- Studio: higher volume and full-service production for teams.

## How it works (one product screen per step)

1. Paste a YouTube or Loom link. It lands on the **Clip board** as a task.
2. The editor picks the best parts, cuts them and adds captions.
3. **Review**: comment on the clip, ask for changes, approve. No Slack, no calls.
4. **Scheduler**: connect YouTube or TikTok and schedule the post from the app (a calendar view).
