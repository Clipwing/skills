# The reference style

Four videos of the Lera Clipwing YouTube channel (16:9, 1.5–3.5 min, a presenter at home talking to the camera):
`jxC64mTQaGk`, `R7s5OOHmo0U`, `fzjGXEIiFXw`, `_O59yBhE5f8`. Measured and read off contact sheets (2026-11-09).

## Rhythm
- A hard cut every **2.9–3.7 s** on average (scene detection: 22 cuts in 84 s, 30 in 88 s, 36 in 134 s), plus framing
  changes inside the presenter's shots that scene detection does not count.
- The presenter alternates **wide** (head and shoulders, room visible) and **close** (face fills the frame). A punch-in
  lands on a stressed word or a reaction ("Nope.").
- Roughly every 10 s an idea leaves the presenter for a full-screen picture of 2–6 s, then cuts back.

## Full-screen pictures
- **Warm paper cards** (`#F4F2EE`): a big serif headline typed letter by letter ("SIMPLE WORDS",
  "How would you search for it?"); checklists with a small icon, a bold title and green ticks, where the **future items
  are already there but blurred** and turn sharp as they are said; good / bad columns with ticks and crosses; a yellow
  marker under a title.
- **UI mockups**: a search bar typing a query with a suggestion list over a grid of video thumbnails, analytics cards
  ("This video has gotten 2,008 views"), all slightly tilted and slowly pushing in.
- **Diagrams**: a mind map of coloured pills around a cyan hub, connected by thin red lines.
- **Quotes**: one short sentence in white over the presenter blurred and dimmed ("Rhythm = engagement").
- Screenshots and product shots on white with a soft shadow; cut-out people on white (not reproduced: needs real
  photos).
- Movie and meme clips (not reproduced: copyright).

## On the presenter
- Big bold sans words beside the head, white with a soft shadow, a small second line under the main word
  ("Unlock / your potential", "Post / Learn / Improve"); they pop in with a short scale and blur.
- A Subscribe button clicked by a cursor at the end.
- No running captions in the 16:9 originals.

## Transitions and VFX
- Whip pans with heavy horizontal motion blur, zoom blur, a warm light leak (orange and pink) on topic changes, a
  quick white flash.
- The camera never stands still: slow push-ins on cards, a slight handheld drift on the presenter.
- Clean, warm grade; light film grain.

## Sound
- Quiet lo-fi / soft beat music under the whole video, lower under speech.
- Whooshes on every transition, pops on items and words, keyboard typing on the search bar, a click on Subscribe.
